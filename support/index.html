Common questions

Where is my trip data stored?

Each trip is a Google Sheet in the Google Drive of the person who created it, and receipt photos go in a “TuTeMeMe Receipts” folder there. We run no server and cannot see your data. See the privacy policy.

How do I sign in?

Tap Sign in with Google. Sign-in happens in your browser through Google, so the app never sees your password. If it says access is blocked, ask us to add your Google address; the sign-in screen may still be restricted to approved testers.

How do I add friends to a trip?

Open the trip, go to People, add the person and their Google e-mail, and choose their role. They then see the trip under Open shared trip after pasting the link from Trip settings → Share link.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Add expenses, payments and comments without a connection. They are kept on your device and sent to the sheet when you are back online. Anything that changes people, members or settings needs a connection.

Some friends do not use the app. Can they see the balances?

An admin can create a read-only link in Trip settings. Anyone with the link sees who owes whom and the totals, and nothing else. You can stop sharing at any time.

How do I back up a trip, or move it?

On the Balances tab choose Export → Backup file. To restore it, use My trips → ⋯ → Restore from backup. A restore always creates a new trip with you as its admin.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the trip’s Google Sheet (and the receipts folder) in Google Drive. To remove the app’s access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out of the app removes the sign-in from your device.

How do I change the language?

My trips → ⋯ → Language: English, Hindi or Marathi, or follow your phone.

UPI payment links do not show up.

UPI settle-up is available for trips in Indian rupees. Check that the person has a valid UPI ID under People.
