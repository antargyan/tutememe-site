TuTeMeMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

TuTeMeMe ("the app") helps groups record shared trip expenses and work out who owes whom. It is published by
ANTARGYAN CLOUDWORKS LLP ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

The short version: your trip data lives in your Google account, not on our servers. We run no server for the app, we do not
collect your data, we have no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share your information.

1. How the app works

You sign in with your Google account. The app then reads and writes a Google Sheet for each trip in your Google Drive, and
(optionally) stores receipt photos in a folder in your Google Drive. Everything the app shows you comes from those files and from a
small copy kept on your device so it works offline. We, the publisher, never receive this data.

2. Information the app handles

Google account information. When you sign in, the app receives your Google account email address. It is used to identify
you in the app, to match you to a person in a trip ("my position"), and to control who can edit a trip. The email addresses of a
trip's members are stored in that trip's Google Sheet, which is shared with those members.

Trip data you enter. Trip names, people's names, expenses (description, amount, currency, date, category, who paid, who shared),
payments, comments, notes, budgets and settings. This is stored in the Google Sheet for the trip, in the Google Drive of the person
who created it, and is visible to the people you add to the trip. Please only enter information that you and the other members are
comfortable sharing with each other.

Receipt photos. If you attach a receipt photo (taken with a camera or chosen from your files), the app shrinks it and uploads it
to a "TuTeMeMe Receipts" folder in your Google Drive. It is shared read-only with the other members of that trip. Until the upload
finishes, the photo is kept in the app's private folder on your device.

Optional public link. An administrator can switch on a read-only public summary link for a trip. Anyone with that link can
see the summary (names and amounts). It can be turned off again in the trip's settings.

Information on your device. To work offline the app keeps on your device: a cached copy of your trips, a queue of changes
waiting to be sent, receipt photos waiting to be uploaded, your settings (such as language and notification choices), and your
Google sign-in tokens. The tokens are kept in the operating system's protected storage.

Exchange rates. If you ask the app for today's exchange rate, it sends only the two currency codes (for example USD and INR)
to the public exchange-rate service api.frankfurter.dev. No personal information is sent. You can always type a rate in by hand.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, usage
analytics or crash reports. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code.

3. Google user data and the Google API Services User Data Policy

The app asks for these Google permissions:

  • Google Sheets and Google Drive (drive.file), to create and update the trip sheets and receipt files that the app itself
    creates or that you open with it. The drive.file permission does not let the app see your other Drive files.
  • Email address (userinfo.email) and OpenID, to know which Google account you are.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use
requirements. In particular, the information is used only to provide the features you see in the app; it is not transferred to
anyone else except as needed to provide those features (for example, trip members you added can see the trip's sheet and receipts),
it is not used for advertising, and no humans read it, because it never reaches us.

4. Who can see your data

  • You, and the people you add to a trip: they can see that trip's sheet and receipts, according to the access you give them.
  • Google, which stores the sheets and files under your Google account and its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Anyone with a public link, only if you switch one on for a trip.
  • Messaging apps, only when you choose to share something (for example a payment reminder through WhatsApp or Telegram). The
    text you share is handed to the app you pick.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or hand it to anyone else.

5. Notifications

If you turn notifications on, the app shows notifications on your device when someone else adds an expense or payment to one of your
trips. These are created on your device by the app; no notification service of ours is involved.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays in your Google account until you delete it. To remove a trip, delete its Google Sheet (and the receipts folder)
    in Google Drive.
  • Signing out of the app removes the sign-in tokens from your device. Uninstalling the app removes the cached copy.
  • You can withdraw the app's access to your Google account at any time at
    myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Because we do not hold your data, we cannot delete it for you, but the steps above remove it. For any request, contact us at the
    address below.

7. Security

Sign-in uses Google's standard OAuth flow in your web browser; the app never sees your Google password. Sign-in tokens are stored
using your device's protected storage, and all communication with Google and the exchange-rate service uses HTTPS. No method of
storage or transfer is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for who you add to a trip and for keeping your device and Google
account secure.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from
them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and for significant changes we will say so in the app
or on the app's Store page.
